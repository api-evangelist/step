# Step

> Step is a free banking platform built for teens, young adults, and families, offering a credit-building Visa card, cashback, savings rewards, and early paycheck access.

Step is available to U.S. users of all ages — users under 18 sign up with a parent who manages account access, while users 18 and over can hold accounts solo. Banking services are provided by Evolve Bank & Trust, Member FDIC, with deposits insured up to $250,000 (up to $1,000,000 for eligible Step Black accounts). The Step Visa Card is a secured Visa accepted anywhere Visa is accepted; Step's Smart Pay automatically settles each month's purchases, building positive payment history that's reported to Experian, Equifax, and TransUnion once a user turns 18 — and teens can build credit history before they turn 18. Step users in their 20s see an average 57-point credit score increase in their first year, Step users start with an average credit score of 721, and Step members who start building credit with Step begin 125 points above the national average. Step Black is free with a qualifying direct deposit or $4.99/mo and unlocks 3.00% savings rewards, up to 10% cashback at partner brands (1% unlimited on everything else), Step EarlyPay (up to $250 before payday with no interest), and $1,000,000 in FDIC coverage. There are no monthly, overdraft, minimum, or account-management fees on Step accounts.
